What ships

Four moves in Pulse 2.0

All four are live for beta teams today and reach every plan on 4 March.

  • 01

    Live board

    Every job, crew and SLA on one screen, refreshed every 15 seconds.

  • 02

    Slip-risk alerts

    Pulse flags the three jobs most likely to slip — before they do.

  • 03

    One-tap handoff

    Reassign straight from the alert; the new crew has it in under a minute.

  • 04

    Offline first

    Crews keep working with no signal and sync the moment they are back.
