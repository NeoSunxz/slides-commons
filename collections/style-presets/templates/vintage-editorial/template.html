Part III · What we changed

Four things we stopped doing

Nobody asked us to. We just felt better afterwards.

  1. 1

    Breaking news

    It was already broken by the time we got there.

  2. 2

    Headlines with questions

    The answer was always “no”, and you knew it.

  3. 3

    Sending on Mondays

    Sunday morning, with coffee, is when you actually read.

  4. 4

    Counting clicks

    We count finished stories now. Fewer, and far nicer.

Part III · What we changed

Promises, kept and otherwise

What we promised

Jan 2026
  • One long story every Sunday
  • A printed edition, twice a year
  • An app, “if readers want one”

What actually happened

Dec 2026
  • 49 of 52 Sundays. The flu won three.
  • One printed edition. It was very heavy.
  • No app. Readers, it turns out, did not.
