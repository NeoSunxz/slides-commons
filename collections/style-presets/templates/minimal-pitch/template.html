Solution

Why now

Why now

Three things changed in the last two years.

  1. 01

    Sales data is open

    Cloud point-of-sale systems now expose item-level sales through public APIs.

  2. 02

    Food costs jumped

    Ingredient prices rose about a quarter since 2022; waste now hurts every week.

  3. 03

    Forecasts got cheap

    A model per restaurant runs for cents a day, not a data team's salary.
