The autumn collection

Four scents, one hour

Each is built on a single garden note and poured in batches of two hundred.

  1. i

    Fig Leaf

    Green, milky and a little bitter, like a branch snapped in late August.

    Heart · fig, cedar

  2. ii

    Wet Stone

    The garden wall after rain: mineral, cool, and almost not there.

    Heart · ambrette, iris

  3. iii

    Orris Butter

    Powdery and warm, the slowest of the four to open on skin.

    Heart · orris, musk

  4. iv

    Smoked Tea

    A lapsang note from the potting shed, softened with honey.

    Heart · tea, benzoin
