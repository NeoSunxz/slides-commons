The week, day by day

Five days, five small wins

Every day ends at 4 pm with a short check-in with your buddy.

  1. Mon

    Laptop, coffee, hello

    Set-up runs from 9:30; lunch with the design team at 12:30.

  2. Tue

    Shadow a design review

    Sit in, listen, and post one question in the review notes.

  3. Wed

    Pick your first ticket

    Choose from the “good first fix” board with your buddy.

  4. Thu

    Pair on the change

    Two hours with an engineer; open the pull request together.

  5. Fri

    Ship it and show it

    Release by 2 pm, then a two-minute demo at studio drinks.

How we work

Small habits, kept on purpose

We do

  • Write it down first. A one-page note before any meeting.
  • Ship small, ship often. Most changes go out the same week.
  • Review kindly. Comments say what to try, not just what’s wrong.

We skip

  • Status meetings. Updates live in the Friday note instead.
  • Big-bang launches. No release waits on another team’s.
  • Late-night replies. Messages after 6 pm can wait until morning.
