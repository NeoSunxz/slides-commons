i. A letter from the publisher

Fewer books, longer lives

This autumn we are publishing eleven books instead of the usual sixteen. It was not an easy decision, and it was not about cost. It was about time: the time each book gets on your front table, and the time we can give it after publication day.

Last year our best-selling title sold more in its second year than its first, almost entirely through bookshops that kept it face out. We want every book on this list to have that chance.

So: fewer launches, longer campaigns, reading copies earlier, and a second push for every title in the spring. We hope you will hold us to it.

— Margot Ellery
