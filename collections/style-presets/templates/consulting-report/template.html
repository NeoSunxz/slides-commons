Preliminary

Margin can recover from 2.8% to 8.5% by FY26 if Meridian consolidates four depots and indexes energy costs in its contracts

Situation

Meridian is the second-largest regional cold-chain operator: €412m revenue, 11 depots and 1,900 customer contracts.

  • Volume grew 5.2% a year FY21–FY24, ahead of a 3.8% market
  • Customer retention is 94%; the top 40 customers are 52% of revenue
€412mFY24 revenue

Complication

Operating margin fell from 8.9% to 2.8% as energy, labour and empty running outpaced pricing.

  • Energy cost per pallet nearly doubled (+94%); 62% of contracts carry no energy indexation
  • Four depots below 60% utilisation lose €4.6m a year
−6.1 ptsMargin change FY21–FY24

Resolution

Consolidate four under-used depots, index energy in contracts and re-plan routes for backhaul.

  • Option B: €23.6m annual EBIT uplift at run rate for €31m one-off capital
  • Three decisions are needed today to start savings in Q2 2025
+5.7 ptsMargin uplift by FY26

Source: Meridian management accounts FY21–FY24; North Quay analysis. Demo data.

What drove the shift

Interviews and cost ledger, 11 depots

1

Energy tariff reset in FY23 at +84% per kWh; 62% of contracts carry no indexation clause, so none of it was passed on.

2

Overtime reached 14% of hours against a 6% benchmark, driven by unplanned peaks at four depots.

3

Fleet and facility shares fell only because energy and labour grew faster; their cost per pallet is flat in euro terms.
