02 · Kids
Playtest notes

Four things kids told us

Paraphrased from parent diaries and our own very sticky notebooks.

  • “Fewer bits, please”

    Projects with under 10 pieces were finished twice as often.

  • “I want to keep it”

    Things that live on a shelf beat things that go in the bin.

  • “Can I do it alone?”

    Five-year-olds asked for picture-only steps, no reading.

  • “Where are the scissors?”

    Every box needs its own tools, not the kitchen drawer’s.
