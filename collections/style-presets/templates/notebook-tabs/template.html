The headline finding

Most visitors never open a library book. They come for a quiet seat, a plug and no time limit.

Seat-level observation, 1,214 visits, 3 February – 6 April 2027.

What we heard

Four needs, in their own words

Grouped from 46 interviews; each card was raised by at least a third of people.

  1. i.

    Stay all day

    “I need to know nobody will ask me to move after two hours.”

  2. ii.

    Power at every seat

    “I pick my seat by where the sockets are, not the light.”

  3. iii.

    Real silence

    “Quiet means no calls. Whispering is fine, phones are not.”

  4. iv.

    Somewhere to eat

    “I leave at lunch and lose my seat. So I don't eat.”

Patterns

What we assumed, what we found

We assumed
  • Visitors are mostly students
  • Busiest in exam season
  • People want more books on the shelves
  • Two-hour limits keep seats fair
We found
  • 58% are remote workers or job-seekers
  • Full every weekday from 10 am
  • People want power, light and tables
  • Limits push people out at lunch
